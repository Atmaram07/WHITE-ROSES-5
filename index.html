05 CONSTRUCTION & FINISHES

SPECIFICATIONS FROM THE BROCHURE

Thoughtful in
the details.

Material and finish descriptions below follow the supplied brochure. Final brands, models, scope and any revisions should be confirmed in the project agreement and specifications schedule.

01

Structure

RCC framed structure with infill brick walls.

02

Flooring

Premium vitrified tiles in living, dining and bedrooms; vitrified tiles in balconies. Granite staircase risers and treads, with stainless-steel railing.

03

Wall finish

Interior: superior acrylic emulsion paint over wall putty. Exterior: weather-resistant paint.

04

Doors & windows

Sal wood door frames; teak-veneered main door with stainless-steel hardware, night latch and eye-piece; laminated flush internal doors; UPVC windows with protective grills.

05

Kitchen

Vitrified tile flooring, granite platform with stainless-steel sink, ceramic/vitrified wall tiles up to 7 ft above the platform, and premium CP fittings.

06

Toilets

Anti-skid ceramic floor tiles, ceramic wall tiles, CP fittings with hot- and cold-water supply provision, and white vitreous sanitary ware.

07

Electricals

Concealed copper wiring in PVC conduits, modular switches, distribution board with MCB/RCCB, and earthing as per IS 3043.

08

Additional provisions

Adequate light and power points in rooms, toilets and kitchen, as stated in the brochure.

Ask for project details ↗
Where is White Roses@5 located?+

The brochure identifies the project as being at Brahmanjharilo, adjacent to Nakhara, Cuttack.

What type of homes are presented?+

4.5 BHK triplex villas, as promoted in the project campaign material.

What are the brochure-listed areas?+

The south-facing typical plan lists a total area of 3,127 sq ft; the north-facing typical plan lists 3,095 sq ft. Refer to the floor-plan section for floor-wise statements.

Does each unit have a lift?+

The project material promotes individual lift access for each unit, and the typical floor plans show a lift.

What is the introductory price?+

The earlier supplied campaign creative states ₹1.45 Cr* for the first 10 units. Confirm current price, terms and availability with the developer.

Which amenities are listed?+

The brochure lists a temple, clubhouse, swimming pool, gym/fitness centre, jogging track, indoor games, badminton court, AC conference hall, kids' play area, senior citizen hangout zone and additional community features.

What construction and finish specifications are mentioned?+

The brochure includes an RCC framed structure with infill brick walls, vitrified tile flooring, specified wall finishes, doors and windows, kitchen and toilet finishes, and electrical provisions. See the specifications section for details.

How long are nearby landmarks from the project?+

The brochure provides estimated drive times to nearby hospitals, schools, shopping destinations, Bhubaneswar Railway Station and Biju Patnaik International Airport. Times may vary with traffic and route.

Can I request a floor plan or arrange a visit?+

Use the enquiry form to prepare a WhatsApp request for a floor plan, project information or a site visit. Review the prepared message and press Send in WhatsApp to complete your enquiry.

What is the current approval status?+

The published disclaimer states that the project is approved for sub-division of layout only, and separate approvals will be obtained from relevant authorities for buildings and amenities. Request current documentation from the developer.
